## Bounds and review authority

Language sampling takes at most 2,000 characters from each of up to 50 pages
spread through the document, capped at 100,000 characters. The detector requires
at least 80 words, 300 letters, distinct diagnostic markers and distributed
agreement; it compares primary language subtags, not regional pronunciation.
Sampling, extraction gaps and supported-language resemblance remain disclosed.
An agreement is a local screen result, not proof that every passage is correct.
No AI, OCR, download, external detector or automatic language overwrite is used.

Contrast measures at most the first 100 pages and 2,000 text regions, with
explicit input/object/render/sample limits. Rotated, transparent, patterned,
image/Form, clipped, overlapping or ambiguous paint stays unmeasured. It cannot
assess image/outlined text or all graphics. Counters bound the declared local
work, not all native PDFium decoding or allocation; that limitation is retained
in evidence. A measured pass is never a complete document contrast pass.

Heading/table controls are collapsed by default and filtered to the previewed
page. Eligibility requires unambiguous content references in the prepared PDF.
Unsafe controls are disabled with a reason. Workspace generations prevent obsolete responses from replacing a later view
or redirecting a follow-up approval; active jobs keep mutable controls disabled
through navigation/filter changes. Draft changes never approve or apply themselves; named acceptance and a separate Apply create/check a new PDF.
The automatic title/Figure authorization cannot supply structural approvals.
Existing saved reviews retain their original inventory profile and bindings;
they are not silently expanded. Process the unchanged original separately to
prepare current structure controls where an older review lacks them.
